Simulated network, real engine. The handshakes come from a simulated campus network of 32 clients and 32 servers, generated on the server from the clock. Every verdict is computed by the zerohour engine in a Vercel function. That engine is parity-tested against the C++ sensor on the lab captures. Every four minutes one client is put behind a TLS interceptor for 90 seconds.
